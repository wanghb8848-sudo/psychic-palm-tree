REIMAGINE HOW EDUCATORS PRESENT PHYSICAL CONTENT

Logitech Reach reimagines how educators present physical content. Its flexible pivoting camera head and smooth single-handed vertical and horizontal sliding controls make object and document exploration effortless. Reach delivers immersive learning experiences in science, technology, engineering, art, math, and more that spark curiosity and inspire innovation.

Explore every angle

The Reach camera lets you explore objects from unique vantage points at every angle, expanding lessons to subjects like science, geography, engineering, art, and more.

Intuitive, fluid movement for dynamic lessons

Reach offers intuitive pan, tilt and zoom control with vertical and horizontal sliders, and a pivoting camera so educators can create engaging and dynamic lessons with physical content beyond just documents.

Effortless camera alignment for clearer engagement with content

The yellow dot indicator and grip ring on the pivoting camera ensures constant awareness and control of the camera's orientation so that educators can keep the content level from their point of view making the lesson easier to follow.

  • Developed in collaboration with teachers, Logitech Reach is specifically engineered to suit educational use cases to bring non-digital content to life.Logitech. (2024). Educator and creator collaboration throughout the internal design process via product testing and feedback.

  • 42% of educators reported students in the classroom have trouble seeing images and other material from the other side of the classroom.Kraft, M. A., & Monti-Nussbaum, M. (2021). The big problem with little interruptions to classroom learning. AERA Open, 7.

Portable and versatile to fit any space

Choose between two versatile mounting options to fit your needs: a stable surface base for flexible desk placement or an edge clamp designed for tight spaces or mobile carts. Both bases are available for purchase as accessories, enabling smooth transitions between setups with a single camera.

Seamless compatibility and easy plug-and-play setup

Logitech Reach offers a plug and play experience over USB without additional or proprietary software to download or install. It’s compatible with most computers, including iPads and Chromebooks, that use USB as well as camera apps, conferencing or streaming platforms. You can even utilize it along with your favorite AI tools to create a seamless view of your stream.

With Logitech StreamCam at its core, Reach delivers 1080p/60fps video quality, crisp dual mic-based audio and glass optics and smart autofocus highlight even the finest details with sharpness and accuracy.Its innovative articulating arms provide two versatile modes: desktop mode for hands-free content exploration and capture, and stowed mode for presenter view or compact storage.
